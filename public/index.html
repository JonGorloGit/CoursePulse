INSTRUCTOR TUTORIAL

From activity to classroom discussion.

CoursePulse collects student contributions and turns them into an anonymous, collective view of the room. It is designed for reflection and discussion — not individual assessment.

CoursePulse does not grade student work.It surfaces patterns, questions, tensions, uncertainties, unusual perspectives and opportunities for discussion.
01

Create an activity

Open the Instructor dashboard and choose + New activity. Use an Open Activity for flexible classroom tasks or a Story Investigation for a structured case such as Nele Brandt.

Student task tells students what to do. Course context / learning goals gives the AI context for the later synthesis.
02

Choose your dashboard

Select the dashboard boxes you expect to use. Themes, tensions, open questions, surprising readings and other views can be switched on or off later as well.

The full pulse is generated in the background, so you can reveal another dashboard box during class without running the analysis again.
03

Invite the room

Every activity has an activity code and a student link. Copy the link or download its QR code from the instructor view and place it in your slides.

Students contribute to the room. CoursePulse does not display an individual score or ranking.
04

Generate the room pulse

Open the activity and select Generate room pulse. CoursePulse reads the submissions collectively and creates the dashboard. All result boxes initially stay collapsed so you decide what to reveal.

Refresh pulse reruns the analysis using the current submissions, Student Task and Course Context.
05

Show the evidence

Use Show examples to reveal up to two short, verbatim excerpts from student responses that support selected dashboard findings.

Examples are not rewritten by the model. Long excerpts may only be shortened with “…”. Turn examples off when you want to discuss the synthesis first.
06

Use the dashboard live

Click a box to expand it, activate or deactivate categories under Dashboard boxes, and use Ask the room for a question that should be answered from the collected submissions.

A useful flow is: reveal a pattern → ask students whether they recognize it → show examples → open a tension or discussion starter.
07

Edit when needed

In the Instructor dashboard, Edit opens the activity directly underneath its card. You can change its title, Student Task or Course Context without deleting submissions.

An existing pulse does not change automatically. Select Refresh pulse when you want the edited instructions to affect the analysis.
08

Manage activities

Use Copy link and Download QR whenever you need to share an activity again. Delete permanently removes the activity, its submissions and its generated pulse after confirmation.

Keep an activity when you only want to change its wording or dashboard layout — both can be edited later.
